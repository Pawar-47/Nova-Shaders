Nova Shaders includes small adaptations from the following MIT-licensed projects. Their complete source licenses are reproduced below.

Glimmer
https://github.com/jbritain/glimmer-shaders
Copyright (c) 2024 Josh Britain
Adapted in shaders/lib/water.glsl: the compact directional wave profile and analytic slope from shaders/lib/water/waveNormals.glsl, and Beer-Lambert water attenuation/in-scattering from shaders/lib/water/waterFog.glsl.

The Glimmer wave-normal source credits "Very fast procedural ocean" by afl_ext:
https://www.shadertoy.com/view/MdXyzX
That upstream method is also identified as MIT-licensed in Glimmer's source.

Spectrum
https://github.com/zombye/spectrum
Copyright (c) 2017-2018 Jacob Eriksson
Adapted in shaders/lib/sky.glsl: the height-profiled cloud-density approach and compact anisotropic phase-scattering model from shaders/include/fragment/clouds3D.fsh. Spectrum's 3D noise textures, compute passes, and multi-ray march are not included.
